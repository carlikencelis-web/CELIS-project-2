Kindergarten to Grade 6

Elementary Department

K–6

The Elementary Department welcomes learners from Kindergarten through Grade 6 into a caring, Christ-centered environment. Here, strong academics go hand in hand with Catholic values, helping students grow both in the classroom and in their faith.

Our teachers know each child by name. With small class sizes, updated facilities, and a close-knit school community, every student gets the attention and encouragement they need.

Why Choose KCC Elementary?

  • Catholic values integrated in all subjects
  • DepEd-aligned K-12 curriculum
  • Small class sizes for personalized learning
  • Dedicated and experienced faculty
  • Safe and nurturing campus environment
  • Regular spiritual formation activities
  • Co-curricular and extracurricular programs
  • Parent involvement and engagement

Strong Academic Foundation

A comprehensive K-6 curriculum aligned with DepEd standards, integrating Catholic values in every subject area.

Faith Formation

Regular liturgical celebrations, prayer services, and Christian Living classes that nurture spiritual growth from an early age.

Arts & Creativity

Programs in visual arts, music, and performing arts that develop creative expression and artistic appreciation.

Math & Science

Engaging math and science instruction using hands-on activities and inquiry-based learning approaches.

Language Proficiency

Strong emphasis on English and Filipino communication skills, with reading enrichment programs.

Character Development

Values education integrated across all learning areas, building responsible and compassionate young learners.

Grades 7 to 10

Junior High School

7–10

The Junior High School Department covers Grades 7 through 10, building on the elementary years and preparing students for Senior High. We follow the DepEd K-12 curriculum while keeping Catholic faith formation at the center.

Students take part in academic competitions, sports, cultural events, and community outreach—gaining experiences that shape them into confident, well-rounded young people.

Learning Areas (Grades 7–10)

  • English
  • Mathematics
  • Science
  • Filipino
  • Araling Panlipunan
  • MAPEH (Music, Arts, PE, Health)
  • Edukasyon sa Pagpapakatao (EsP)
  • Technology and Livelihood Education (TLE)
  • Christian Living / Religious Education

Holistic Curriculum

A well-rounded JHS program following the K-12 Enhanced Basic Education curriculum with Catholic integration.

STEM Readiness

Strong foundation in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics to prepare students for SHS tracks.

Research & Innovation

Introduction to basic research methodologies, encouraging curiosity and scientific inquiry among students.

Co-Curricular Activities

Scouting, campus journalism, sports, quiz bees, and various competitions at division and regional levels.

Spiritual Programs

Retreats, recollections, masses, and community outreach that form students in Christian service and values.

Student Leadership

Supreme Student Government and class organizations that develop leadership skills and civic responsibility.
